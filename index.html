Questions

Things people ask before booking.

Four quick answers. For the full list and more questions, see the full FAQ.

Who's the ideal Avoxan client?
Service businesses doing $250K–$5M a year. Coaches, consultants, dentists, lawyers, real-estate teams, photographers, contractors, fitness studios, accountants. People who get clients through their website and need it to actually work, not just exist.
Why is your pricing lower than other agencies?
Two reasons. First, we're a small studio with no overhead, no Class-A office, no eight-person account team, no $40K Salesforce contract. Second, we're still building our portfolio, so we've priced aggressively to attract clients we can do incredible work for. This pricing will not be here forever.
What platforms do you build on?
Webflow, WordPress (with Elementor or a custom block theme), or Framer, your choice. We avoid Wix and Squarespace because they're harder to grow into. If you have a preference based on your existing setup, we'll match it.
What if I hate it?
You won't, but if you do, that's why the 14-day money back exists. After the first design round, if you're not feeling it, we refund everything and walk away on good terms. Has happened exactly zero times so far, but the guarantee is real.
