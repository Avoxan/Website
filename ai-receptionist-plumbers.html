The cost of a missed call

Why missed calls are expensive.

When a customer calls and nobody answers, they usually just call the next company on Google. Here's a simple way to picture the missed opportunity.

8 missed calls/week × $350 avg job × 52 weeks × 30% who'd have booked

= $43,680 / year in potential lost revenue

This is an illustration of potential lost revenue, not a guarantee. Every business is different. Even so, the point holds: Avoxan costs a fraction of one lost job per month.

Questions

Straight answers before you book.

It backs up your team when they're busy, unavailable, or closed, complex situations escalate so you stay in control.

Do I need to change my phone number?
No. Your existing number stays the same. Your phone rings first. If you miss the call, Avoxan answers as your safety net.
Can it answer after-hours calls?
Yes. Avoxan can answer at night, on weekends, and during busy periods when your team cannot pick up.
What information does it collect?
Name, phone number, service address, issue, urgency, and best callback time. For plumbing calls, it can also flag active leaks, burst pipes, no hot water, and emergency situations.
Can it book appointments?
Yes, appointment booking can be added if your business uses a calendar or booking process that can be connected. Most plumbing companies start with call capture and lead summaries first.
Will customers know it is AI?
Avoxan is designed to sound natural, professional, and clear. Its job is not to pretend to be human. Its job is to answer quickly, collect the right details, and help your team follow up faster.
What happens if the caller has a complex question?
Avoxan collects the details and sends them to your team. Your team stays in control of anything complex.
Does it filter spam calls?
Yes. Avoxan can screen obvious spam, wrong numbers, and irrelevant calls so they do not get treated like real leads. No spam filter is perfect, but the goal is to help your team focus on real customer calls.
Is there a contract?
No long-term contract for founding clients. It is month-to-month with a 28-day money-back guarantee.
How fast can it go live?
Most simple setups can go live quickly after we collect your business details, call-handling rules, and notification preferences.
