Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

What does AI receptionist setup involve, and how long does it take?

About a 30-minute interview about your business. We write the script, load your services, prices, hours and service area, handle the carrier registration that business texting now requires, and test it with real calls before it goes live. Most businesses are live within a week. Setup is free on every plan.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You forward missed or after-hours calls from your existing number, so customers keep calling the number they already have.

What happens if I go over my included minutes?

Included minutes are a guide, not a cap. The receptionist keeps answering and there is no per-minute overage billing. If you run over every month, we talk to you about the plan that fits before any price changes.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

It answers as your business's assistant, and if a caller asks, it says plainly that it is an AI assistant. On Answer and Front Desk, urgent callers can be transferred straight to your phone.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?

If your AI receptionist hasn't brought you at least one new lead in its first 30 days, tell us and we refund your first month in full.

What's the founding rate?

The first 10 AI receptionist clients keep the price they sign up at for as long as they stay with us, even after our prices rise.

Is the website price really fixed?

Yes. The Business Website is a flat $2,400 for eight pages with every deliverable included, paid 50% to start and 50% at launch. Extra pages are $150 each. A Custom Build, from $5,000, is a different kind of project for logins, booking or a storefront.

Can I combine a website and an AI receptionist?

Yes, and it's the setup we recommend. Website clients get the first two months of any AI receptionist plan free.
