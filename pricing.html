Why this price

How a $5,400 retail project becomes a $1,500 flat fee.

01

We have no overhead.

No Class-A office in downtown Houston. No eight-person account team. No $48K-a-year Salesforce contract. A small studio means most of what you pay goes into actual design and code, not into a building's lease.

02

We're building a portfolio.

Avoxan launched in early 2026. We need portfolio pieces more than we need margin right now. Aggressive intro pricing is how we attract the right early clients, once we have 30 case studies, this price will be $4,500.

03

We've productized the scope.

Most agencies quote you bespoke because every project is "different." We've found that 90% of small-business sites need the same 8 things. By fixing the scope, we fix the price, and we never have to argue about line items.

04

One owner, one project at a time.

No project managers. No handoffs between a salesperson, a strategist, a designer, a developer, and a QA person. You talk to the person doing the work. That's a 4× efficiency gain right there.

05

We've automated the boring parts.

Schema generation, image optimization, page-speed audits, accessibility checks, sitemap generation, all scripted. The work that an agency junior spends 12 hours on, our pipeline handles in 20 minutes.

06

We make our margin on referrals.

A client who got a great website for $1,500 tells five other business owners. We'd rather under-price the project and over-deliver on the outcome than charge full freight and never hear from you again.
