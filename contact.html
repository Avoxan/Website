Before you book

The call is faster if you've thought through:

  • What does a "great outcome" look like 6 months after launch?
  • Who's your single best customer, and how did they find you?
  • What's the one thing competitors do that you wish you did better?
  • Any platform preference, or are you open?

No homework required. If you have answers, great. If not, that's literally what the call is for.
