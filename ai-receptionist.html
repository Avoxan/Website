The honest part

What it won't do.

An AI receptionist is good at a specific job. We set it up to do that job well and to hand off the rest, instead of bluffing.

  • Pretend to be human. If a caller asks, it says plainly that it's an AI assistant for your business.
  • Quote prices you haven't approved. It only gives the prices and ranges you've given us, and says “the tech will confirm on site” for the rest.
  • Diagnose, or give medical or legal advice. It takes the details and gets a qualified person to call back.
  • Make up an answer. When it doesn't know, it says so, takes a message and flags it for you.
  • Lock you in. Month to month. Turn off call forwarding and it stops, the same day.

Questions

Before you switch it on.

Does an AI receptionist sound robotic?

No. Press play on the call above: it speaks naturally, with normal pauses, and keeps up with callers who interrupt or change their mind. It isn't trying to fool anyone, though. If someone asks, it tells them it's an AI assistant.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Our plans are $97 a month for missed-call text-back (Catch), $197 for voice answering in English and Spanish (Answer), and $397 for booking, reminders and reviews (Front Desk). Setup is free, there's no contract, and yearly billing gets you two months free.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It doesn't guess. It tells the caller someone will get back to them, takes a detailed message and flags it in your summary. Urgent calls can be transferred to your cell instead.

Can I keep my business phone number?

Yes. You forward calls from your existing number, on the schedule you choose: only missed calls, after hours, when the line is busy, or every call. Your customers keep dialing the number they already know.

Do I need a website to use it?

No. It works with just your phone number. Front Desk includes a free one-page website, and if you build a full site with us, the first two months of any AI plan are free.

Is the AI receptionist really available 24/7?

Yes, including nights, weekends and holidays. It can answer several calls at once, so a busy morning never sends anyone to voicemail.

What happens if I go over my included minutes?

Nothing breaks and there's no overage fee. Included minutes are a guide, not a cap. If you run over every month, we'll talk about the right plan before anything changes.

Is it legal to record calls in Texas?

Texas is a one-party consent state, and the receptionist also tells callers the call may be recorded. If you take calls from states that require everyone's consent, that notice covers you too.

Can it work with my scheduling software?

Front Desk books into Google Calendar and Outlook out of the box. For field-service and booking software like Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Jane or Zenoti, we connect it as part of a custom setup, from $1,500.
