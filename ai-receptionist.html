The honest part

The call goes to voicemail, and the caller just dials the next business.

Your team is on a job, with a client, in a treatment room, or closed for the night. The phone rings, nobody can answer, it rolls to voicemail, and most people don't leave one. They hang up and call the next name on the list. That caller is gone, and you never even knew they rang.

For a service business, one missed call can be a booked job, a new patient, a consultation, or a high-value appointment. A handful of missed calls a week can quietly cost more than any software subscription.

  • Calls that used to hit voicemail while you're on a job now get answered live.
  • After-hours and weekend callers get captured instead of slipping away.
  • Leads who'd never leave a message get caught and qualified.
  • Spam and robocalls get screened out, so they stop eating your time.
  • Every call gets logged, who rang, when, and what they wanted.

Questions

What people ask before turning it on.

Honest answers about how it sounds, what it can't do, and whether you even need a website to use it.

Does it sound robotic?
Avoxan is designed to sound natural, professional, and clear with natural conversational pauses. Its job is not to pretend to be human. Its job is to answer quickly, collect the right details, and help your team follow up faster.
What if it can't answer something?
It doesn't bluff. If a caller asks something outside what it knows, it takes a detailed message or escalates, it can forward to your cell or promise a callback and text you immediately. You're never trading a missed call for a wrong answer.
Do I need a website to use this?
No. The receptionist works on its own with just your phone number. It pairs nicely with an Avoxan website, the two share one lead inbox, but plenty of businesses start with the receptionist alone and add a site later.
Is it really 24/7?
Yes. It answers nights, weekends, and holidays with no shift to cover and no overtime. You decide whether it takes every call or only the ones you miss, but it's available around the clock either way.
Can I keep my current number?
Yes. You keep your existing business number. We set up call forwarding so the AI answers on your terms, all calls, after-hours only, or just the ones you don't pick up. Nothing about your number changes for your customers.
