Bag Chasers Laundry Co.

A laundry pickup service that books, routes and bills itself.

Bag Chasers Laundry Co.
Industry
Wash-and-fold pickup & delivery

Website, booking engine, customer accounts, local SEO
Built with
Static build on Cloudflare Pages, CleanCloud API
Size
29 pages, 12 towns
Live at
bagchaserslaundry.com ↗

The brief

A new laundry pickup business needed more than a brochure. Customers had to check their ZIP, book a window, store a card and manage their orders, and every booking had to reach the shop's point-of-sale system without anyone retyping it.

Our approach

We treated the booking flow as the product and the marketing pages as its front door. Every price comes from one rate card and every town from one list, so the site can grow without ever contradicting itself.

Booking

The van already knows your street.

Five steps from address to confirmed pickup, with a running estimate. The scheduler reads the route and recommends windows when the van is already on your street, at 2% off. The booking lands in CleanCloud as a real pickup, and the customer manages it from their account.

"It would literally do all the logistics for me."

Michael, on what the scheduler needed to do

Local search

Twelve towns, one route.

A ZIP checker answers "do you come to me?" in one field. Four towns have their own pages, written around their own neighborhoods and route days, and a Spanish-language page serves Lilburn. Town and ZIP counts are generated from the same list, so the copy, the sitemap and the map always agree.

Engineering

A price that can't drift.

$2.09 a pound appears on fifteen pages and in the structured data on every one. It lives in one file, and the build refuses to publish if any page states a price that didn't come from it.

The brief

Julie and Cody Wall place group health, dental, vision, life and disability plans for Texas businesses, then service them all year. Clients stay because a person who knows their plan answers the phone. The site had to say that plainly, and carry figures on a subject where a wrong number costs trust.

Our approach

Lead with the two people, write the way they talk, and back every number with its source. A calm "Evergreen & Linen" palette, a serif with some warmth, and pages that explain a benefits package in plain English.

Trust

Numbers with receipts.

Every statistic on the site shows its source and its date. The same data file feeds the visible page and its structured data, so what people read and what search engines read can never disagree.

Our idea

A page for every client.

Not in the brief. A plain-English benefits page for each employer, opened from a QR code at the enrollment meeting and readable in English or Spanish, so employees can go through their options at home instead of losing a PDF.

"It's very professional. I would have never even thought of doing something like that."

Cody Wall, President

Voice

Written around the people.

Julie and Cody are the reason clients stay, so they are on the page in their own photos and close to their own words. Industry pages speak to a machine shop differently from a doctor's office, because the coverage they need is different.

The brief

Brenda prepares divorce, custody, probate and small-claims paperwork for Californians handling their own case. She closes on the phone, so the site has one job: get a nervous visitor to call, text or book a free consult, while staying inside the state's rules for legal document assistants.

Our approach

Give her personality room without making the work look unserious. Plum and ivory carry the page, pink is kept for the call to action, and the glamour is in the staging, never in the claims.

Art direction

The Parlor.

She loves Victorian homes and 1950s glamour, so we built a world of velvet, gilt and lamplight. Services read like a theater program, how it works plays out in three acts, and the booking band is a small stage with its curtains tied back.

Imagery

Every page, its own scene.

Each page opens on its own art-directed still life: a cash register for Pricing, a rotary phone for Contact, a vintage convertible for Los Angeles County. No stock photography and not one gavel.

Compliance & control

Inside the rules, in her hands.

Every footer carries California's required disclosures in English and Spanish. Prices, court fees, hours and FAQs live in a content editor she uses herself, and a fee changed once updates every answer that quotes it.
