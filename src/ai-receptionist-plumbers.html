Made for Houston

When Houston's phones light up.

Plumbing calls here come in waves. A receptionist that answers many calls at once is worth the most on exactly the days you can't answer any of them.

  1. Jan – FebHard freezes

    One freeze can mean a week of burst pipes. Winter Storm Uri in February 2021 left plumbers across the city booked out for weeks.

  2. Mar – MaySpring storms

    Heavy rain and street flooding bring sewer backups and sump pump calls, often all on the same night.

  3. Jun – NovHurricane season

    After Hurricane Beryl in July 2024, many homes lost power for days. Calls about well pumps, water heaters and flood damage pile up while your own lines may be down.

  4. Jul – SepDry heat and slab leaks

    Houston's clay soil shrinks in long dry spells and shifts foundations, which is when slab leak calls climb.

  5. Nov – DecHolidays

    Thanksgiving's “day after” drain calls, full houses and guests. Your office is closed. Your phone shouldn't be.

Questions from plumbers

Straight answers.

Do I need to change my business phone number?

No. Your number stays the same and your phone still rings first. You forward the calls you miss, or your after-hours calls, to the AI with a setting on your phone. Your trucks, yard signs and Google listing don't change.

How does it handle plumbing emergencies at night?

You tell us what counts as an emergency, like a burst pipe, a leak that won't shut off or sewage in the house. Those calls are flagged and transferred straight to your cell, or your on-call tech, with the address texted ahead. Everything else is booked for the morning so your night stays quiet.

Can it quote prices for plumbing jobs?

Only the prices you give it. Many shops let it share a service call fee or a price range for common jobs, and have it say “the plumber will confirm on site” for everything else. It never makes up a number.

What information does it collect from callers?

Name, callback number, service address, the problem in the caller's words, how urgent it is, whether it's a home or a business, and the best time to call back. It can also ask for photos by text, which helps you quote water heaters and leaks before you roll a truck.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes, on the Answer and Front Desk plans. It switches to Spanish when the caller does and sends you the summary in English. In Houston that alone can win jobs other plumbers miss.

Can it book jobs into Housecall Pro, Jobber or ServiceTitan?

Front Desk books into Google Calendar or Outlook out of the box. Booking directly into field-service software is a custom setup, from $1,500, and we'll tell you on the demo call whether it's worth it for your shop.

What does an AI answering service for plumbers cost?

$97 a month for missed-call text-back, $197 for voice answering with emergency transfer, and $397 with booking, reminders and review requests. Setup is free and there's no contract. Compare that to a live answering service, which usually bills $1 to $5 a minute.

Will homeowners know it's an AI?

It sounds natural and answers in your company's name, but if someone asks, it tells them it's an AI assistant. Most callers just want to know when a plumber can come, and it tells them right away.

How fast can it go live?

Usually within a week. We do a 30-minute interview about your services, prices, service area and emergency rules, build and test it, and register your number for business texting.
