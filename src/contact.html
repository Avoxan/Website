Quick answers.

More in the full FAQ.

Is the first call free?

Yes. It's a free 30-minute conversation about your business. There's no pitch deck and no obligation, and if we're not the right fit we'll tell you who might be.

Do you only work with Houston businesses?

No. We're based in Houston and know the local market well, but most projects run over Zoom, and we work with clients across Texas, the rest of the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day, and usually the same day if you write on a weekday. Booking a time on the calendar is instant.

How do payments work?

Website projects are a fixed price: 50% to start and 50% at launch, by card through Stripe, ACH or wire. AI receptionist plans are billed monthly or yearly, and you can cancel anytime. See pricing.
