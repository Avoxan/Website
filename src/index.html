01 / 03 · Laundry pickup & delivery Gwinnett County, Georgia Live since October 2026

Bag Chasers Laundry Co.

A laundry pickup service that books, routes and bills itself.

  • 29-page website
  • Booking engine
  • CleanCloud integration
  • Customer accounts
  • Local SEO

The brief

A new laundry pickup business in Gwinnett County needed more than a brochure. Customers had to be able to check their ZIP, book a window, store a card and manage their orders, and every booking had to reach the shop's point-of-sale system without anyone retyping it.

What we built

  1. Live booking connected to CleanCloud, the shop's point-of-sale
  2. A route-aware scheduler that recommends the windows when the van is already on your street
  3. Card on file, charged only once the bag is weighed at the door
  4. Customer accounts to track, reschedule and cancel pickups
  5. A build that refuses to publish a page with a price that disagrees with the rate card

02 / 03 · Employee benefits brokerage Houston, Texas 2026

The Clever Insurance

A benefits brokerage whose website now feels like calling them.

  • Brand system
  • Copywriting
  • 13-page website
  • Sourced data & schema
  • Zero-JavaScript build

The brief

Julie and Cody Wall place group health, dental, vision, life and disability plans for Texas businesses, then service them all year. Clients stay because a person who knows their plan answers the phone. The site had to say that plainly, and carry figures on a subject where a wrong number costs trust.

What we built

  1. The "Evergreen & Linen" brand system and a redrawn clover mark
  2. A full copy deck, written from their own words
  3. Every statistic shown with a visible, dated source
  4. One data file that feeds both the page and its structured data, so they can never disagree
  5. A palette audited for contrast on every surface it touches

03 / 03 · Legal document assistant All of California Launching November 2026

theprepdoc

Court paperwork help, staged like a Victorian parlor after hours.

  • Art direction
  • 12-page website
  • Self-serve content editing
  • Booking & payments
  • Compliance

The brief

Brenda prepares divorce, custody, probate and small-claims paperwork for Californians handling their own case. She closes on the phone, so the site has one job: get a nervous visitor to call, text or book a free consult, while staying inside the state's rules for legal document assistants.

What we built

  1. "The Parlor": velvet, gilt and lamplight, drawn from her love of Victorian homes and 1950s glamour
  2. An art-directed still life for every page, with no stock photography
  3. Required disclosures in English and Spanish in every footer
  4. A content editor so she can change prices, court fees, hours and FAQs herself
  5. Booking, intake and payments wired through Cal.com, Tally and Stripe
