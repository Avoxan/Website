Why four weeks holds

Where projects slip, and how we don't.

Most website projects that run two months late fail in the same three places. Each one has a rule in our process.

Slip 01

Skipping the wireframes

Jumping straight to pretty mockups feels fast, until the page structure changes in week three and everything is redrawn. We lock structure on Friday of week one, before a single color is chosen.

Slip 02

Waiting on content

The classic stall is a designer waiting for the client's copy. We write every page ourselves from the discovery call, so there is nothing to wait for.

Slip 03

Endless revision rounds

Two structured rounds, each with a date on the calendar. You review the homepage before the inner pages are built, so feedback lands once instead of eight times.

Before kickoff

What to have ready.

Short list. We'll send it again after you book, and if something's missing we'll work around it.

  • Your logoAny format. If you don't have one worth keeping, ask about brand identity.
  • Photos you ownYour team, your work, your trucks. Real photos beat stock every time.
  • Domain and website accessOr just the name of whoever manages them. We'll handle the rest.
  • Reviews you're proud ofGoogle, Yelp or texts from customers, with their permission.
  • One hour for the discovery callThe most important hour of the project. Everything is written from it.

After launch

The first 90 days.

Launch isn't the end of the project. It's when we start finding out what works.

  1. Day 1Edits open

    Email any change, big or small. Most are done the same day.

  2. Day 14First numbers

    We check that every form, call and booking is being tracked, and send you what we see.

  3. Day 45Search check

    Which pages Google has indexed, which searches you're appearing for, and what to add next.

    No inquiries in 90 days? We audit the conversion path and rebuild the homepage at no cost.

Questions

About the timeline.

How long does it take to build a small business website?

For an 8-page Avoxan website, about four weeks from the discovery call to launch day. Larger custom builds with booking or accounts are scoped separately and usually run longer.

How much of my time does a website project take?

Plan on about four hours across the month: the 60-minute discovery call, then short reviews of the wireframes, the design and the staging site.

What do I need to prepare before we start?

Your logo, any photos you own, access to your domain and current website, and a list of customer reviews you're happy for us to use. We write the copy, so you don't need to.

What happens if I'm slow to give feedback?

The launch date moves by the same number of days, and we'll tell you the new date the moment it changes. Our on-time guarantee covers delays caused by us.

What happens after the website launches?

You get 90 days of free edits, usually done the same day. If the site brings no inquiries in those 90 days, we audit the conversion path and rebuild the homepage at no cost. After that, there's an optional $150/month care plan.
