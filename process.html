Process

From kickoff to launch in 4 weeks.

Five steps. Each step has a fixed deliverable, a fixed deadline, and a clear handoff. Click any step to see the day-by-day breakdown, including the points where most agencies quietly lose two weeks.

01

Discovery call

A structured interview about your business, your customers, your differentiator, and the result you want this website to drive. We record it (with your permission) and transcribe it, every page on the site is later written from this transcript.

Day 0 · 60 min +

What actually happens

  • Minutes 1–15. Your business model. Who pays you, how much, and why they chose you over the next option.
  • Minutes 15–35. Your customer. The exact words a good-fit customer used the last three times they reached out.
  • Minutes 35–50. Your differentiator. The thing competitors do badly that you do well, said in plain English.
  • Minutes 50–60. Objections. The 3–5 reasons people considered you and then didn't book.

Deliverable

A 6–10 page strategy brief emailed to you within 48 hours. You sign off on it before any design starts.

02

Strategy & wireframes

Information architecture, page-by-page wireframes, and a content map. No visual design yet, we lock the structure first because changing structure later is what blows up timelines.

Week 1 +

What gets built

  • A full sitemap with every URL and its purpose
  • Low-fidelity wireframes for every page, desktop, tablet, mobile
  • Copy structure: headline, sub-head, CTA, and section flow for each page
  • SEO keyword map: the 1–3 search queries each page is built to win

Where projects usually break

The temptation is to skip wireframes and go straight to "pretty mockups." We don't. Every project that's been late was late because someone tried to skip this step.

03

Design & copy

High-fidelity design for every page in parallel with the final copy pass. You see the homepage first, approve the design direction, and the rest of the pages follow the same system.

Week 2 +

How we work

  • Day 1–2. Homepage design v1, copy v1, sent for review.
  • Day 3. Round 1 revisions. Usually small.
  • Day 4–7. Inner pages built on the approved system. We deliver them in batches of two.
  • Day 7. Round 2 revisions (final).

What you get

Figma file with every page, every component, every breakpoint. Yours to keep regardless of how the project goes.

04

Build & QA

Development on your chosen platform (Webflow, WordPress, or Framer). Schema markup, page-speed tuning, form integrations, conversion tracking, and a full QA pass across browsers and devices.

Week 3 +

The build checklist

  • All pages built pixel-accurate to the Figma file
  • Schema markup validated with Google's Rich Results Test
  • PageSpeed Insights score 90+ on mobile and desktop
  • Forms tested end-to-end: submission → email → CRM
  • GA4 and conversion events configured and verified
  • Cross-browser QA: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge
  • Device QA: iPhone, Android, iPad, common laptop sizes
  • Accessibility audit: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance check
05

Launch & handoff

Domain pointed, DNS migrated, SSL active, redirects mapped, sitemap submitted to Google Search Console, and you receive a one-page summary of every login and account. Then 90 days of free post-launch edits begin.

Week 4 +

Launch day

  • DNS cutover (we coordinate with your current host if any)
  • Old URLs 301-redirected to new ones, every single one
  • Sitemap submitted to Google, Bing, and indexed within hours
  • Google Business Profile updated with new URL
  • Final invoice sent (50% of the project)

The next 90 days

You email us anytime you want a tweak, copy fixes, new testimonials, a missing page, a typo. Usually turned around same-day. After 90 days, you can transition to a $150/month maintenance retainer or take over yourself.

Our promise to you

Three guarantees most agencies won't put in writing.

01

14-day money back

If after the first design round you're not happy with the direction, we refund every dollar. No "kill fees," no deductions for hours spent. Full stop.

02

On-time or we pay you

If we miss our 4-week launch deadline through any fault of ours, we refund $200 from the final payment. We're allergic to the freelancer-disappearing-act.

03
