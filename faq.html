01Websites

How much does a website cost with Avoxan?

A complete eight-page Business Website is $2,400, one time, with design, copywriting, SEO, hosting setup and 90 days of edits included. A one-page site is free with our Front Desk AI plan, or $67 to set up with the other plans. Projects that need logins, online booking or a storefront are a Custom Build, from $5,000. Every price is on our pricing page.

What's included in the $2,400 website?

Eight things, all in full: a custom homepage, seven supporting pages, copywriting for every page, technical SEO, AI-search optimization, conversion tracking and lead capture, hosting, domain and security setup, and 90 days of free edits after launch. Each one is itemized on the services page.

How long does it take to build a website?

About four weeks from the discovery call to launch day: strategy and wireframes in week one, design and copy in week two, the build and testing in week three, and launch in week four. You'll spend about four hours on it across the month. The process page shows it day by day.

What platforms do you build on?

Webflow, WordPress, Framer, or hand-coded for sites that need to be as fast as possible. We'll recommend one based on who's going to edit the site and what it needs to do. We don't build on Wix or Squarespace, because businesses tend to outgrow them.

Do you write the copy, or do I?

We do. Every page is written by us after a recorded 60-minute interview about your business, so you don't need to show up with anything written. You review it in two rounds. If you need a Spanish version of the site, we set up the translation and work with a translator, which usually adds $400 to $700 depending on length.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds on the design and two on the copy. Most projects use one and a half of each. If you need a small third pass, we'll do it without making it a change order.

Will I see the website before it goes live?

Three times: the wireframes at the end of week one, the design in week two, and the working staging site in week three. Nothing goes live without your written OK.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, and most of our projects are redesigns. We move over the content worth keeping, keep your existing page addresses where we can, and set up redirects for the rest, so you don't lose the Google rankings you already have.

Can you build online booking or an online store?

Yes. A small store with up to 25 products is a $1,200 add-on. Anything bigger, like customer accounts, a custom booking system or multi-location scheduling, is a Custom Build from $5,000. The booking engine we built for Bag Chasers is a good example.

02SEO & search

Will my new website rank on Google?

We build every site to rank: clean code, fast pages, the right page titles, local business schema, and a page for each service you want to be found for. What we won't do is promise a #1 spot, because nobody honest can. For local searches in Houston, most businesses see real movement in three to six months, faster if your Google Business Profile is in good shape.

What is AI-search optimization?

More people now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews who to hire instead of scrolling through results. Those tools quote websites that state facts clearly. We structure your pages and schema, write direct answers to the questions your customers ask, and publish an llms.txt file so AI tools can read your site and cite you correctly.

Do you set up my Google Business Profile?

Yes, it's included. We set up or clean up your profile, match your name, address and phone number across the web, choose the right categories, and connect it to your new site. For local businesses it's often worth as much as the website itself.

Do you offer ongoing SEO?

Yes, as an option. Ongoing SEO content is $700 a month for four researched articles, written and published for the searches your customers make. We'll only suggest it if your market is competitive enough to need it.

Do you do local SEO for Houston suburbs?

Yes. For businesses that serve several areas, like Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland or Cypress, we build location pages with real local detail instead of copy-and-paste city pages, which Google ignores.

03Pricing & payment

Why is your pricing lower than other agencies?

We're a small studio with no downtown lease and no account managers, and we automate the repetitive technical work, so your money pays for design and code. We're also building our portfolio, and our prices are published in advance: $2,400 today, rising to $3,600 once we have 30 published case studies.

How does payment work?

Website projects are 50% to reserve your start date and 50% at launch, in US dollars by card through Stripe, ACH or wire transfer. AI receptionist plans are billed monthly, or yearly with two months free.

Are there any costs on top of the price?

Only the ones you'd pay anyway, billed directly to you so you stay in control: your domain name, usually $10 to $20 a year, and your platform's hosting plan, which runs from nothing for a hand-coded site to roughly $20 to $40 a month for Webflow or managed WordPress. We'll tell you the exact figure before you sign.

What do the add-ons cost?

Extra pages are $150 each. A blog system is $420, e-commerce for up to 25 products is $1,200, and brand identity (logo, type, color palette and guidelines) is $1,000. Monthly maintenance is $150 a month, and ongoing SEO content is $700 a month. All optional, all listed on the pricing page.

If I need more than eight pages, do I have to buy a Custom Build?

No. Extra pages are $150 each, so a 12-page site is $2,400 plus $600. A Custom Build is for websites that need to do something, like logins, booking or a store, not for websites that are simply bigger.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee for my website?

No. After the 90 days of free edits, our care plan is optional at $150 a month for hosting management, security updates, monthly edits and a performance report. Cancel any month. Many clients make their own edits and never need it.

04Ownership & after launch

Who owns the website, the design and the code?

You do, as soon as the final invoice is paid. The design files, the code, the content and every account are in your name. We never hold a website hostage to keep a client.

What happens after the website launches?

You get 90 days of free edits, usually done the same day. We submit your sitemap to Google, watch your tracking, and hand over a document with every login. If the site brings in no inquiries in those 90 days, we audit the path and rebuild the homepage at no cost.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. On Webflow, WordPress and Framer you can edit text, photos, blog posts and team members yourself, and we show you how before we hand it over. For hand-coded sites, we make the edits for you.

What if I don't like the design?

That's why our 14-day money-back guarantee exists. If you're not happy with the direction after the first design round, we refund every dollar, with no deductions for time spent.

What if you miss the launch date?

If we miss the four-week launch because of something on our side, we take $200 off your final payment. If the delay comes from waiting on feedback or content, the date moves by the same number of days and we'll tell you the new date right away.

05AI receptionist

What is an AI receptionist?

It's a voice and text assistant that answers your business phone in your business's name, 24 hours a day. It answers questions, takes the job details, flags emergencies, books appointments and texts you a summary of every call. Ours is set up, tested and looked after by us. Hear how it sounds.

How much does the AI receptionist cost?

Three plans: Catch at $97 a month texts back every missed call, Answer at $197 a month picks up missed, after-hours and overflow calls, and Front Desk at $397 a month also books appointments and asks for reviews. Setup is free and there's no contract. See the AI plans.

Do I need an Avoxan website to use the AI receptionist?

No. It works with your existing phone number and website. If you do have a website with us, the first two months of any AI plan are free.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You forward missed or after-hours calls from the number you already have, so nothing needs reprinting.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

It answers as your business's assistant and sounds natural, but if a caller asks, it says plainly that it's an AI assistant. On the Answer and Front Desk plans, urgent callers can be transferred straight to your cell.

Does the AI receptionist speak Spanish?

Yes. On the Answer and Front Desk plans it answers in English and switches to Spanish when the caller does, which matters a lot in Houston.

What if it doesn't bring in any business?

If your AI receptionist hasn't brought you at least one new lead in its first 30 days, tell us and we'll refund your first month in full.

06Working with us

Who is Avoxan a good fit for?

Owner-led service businesses, roughly $250K to $5M a year, that win customers through their website: contractors, clinics, law firms, med spas, consultants, coaches and local brands. If people search for you before they call you, we can help.

Do you work with brand-new businesses?

Yes, but honestly, if you haven't made your first sales yet, we'll probably suggest a $20-a-month website builder until you have steady customers. Spending $2,400 before you've proven demand isn't the best use of your money, and we'd rather tell you that than take the project.

Where is Avoxan based?

Houston, Texas. Avoxan is a trading name of Akarshan Digital LLC. Most projects run over Zoom, and we work with businesses across Texas, the rest of the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.

Who will I actually work with?

One lead designer runs your project from the first call to launch, and that's who you talk to. There are no account managers passing messages along. Specialists help behind the scenes when a project needs them.

Do you only build websites, or do you also do the AI phone answering?

Both. Websites are our core work, and the AI receptionist is a monthly service that can run on its own or alongside a site we build. When you have both, your website leads and phone leads land in the same place.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute call on our contact page, or send us a note. If we're a good fit, you'll get a fixed quote in writing within 48 hours.
