Platforms

Built on what fits you.

We don't push one platform. We pick the one that suits how you'll run the site after we hand it over.

WebflowBest if you want to edit pages visually and never touch a plugin. Our default for most service businesses.
WordPressBest if you already run it, need a specific plugin or want the widest pool of developers later.
FramerBest for design-led brands that want motion and fast iteration, like photographers and studios.
Hand-codedBest for speed and custom systems. Clever and Bag Chasers are static builds with no framework weight.
Not Wix or SquarespaceFine for getting started, but harder to grow into. We'll tell you if starting there is the smarter move for now.

Questions

Before you ask.

More answers on the full FAQ.

How long does it take to build a small business website?

About four weeks from kickoff to launch for an 8-page Avoxan website. Week one is strategy and wireframes, week two design and copy, week three the build and testing, and week four launch. The process page walks through it day by day.

Do I own my website when it's done?

Yes. The domain, hosting, analytics and every account are set up in your name, and you get a one-page summary of every login at handoff.

Do you write the website copy?

Yes. We write every page from scratch after a 60-minute interview with you, with two rounds of copy revisions included.

Can you redesign my existing website without losing my Google rankings?

Yes. We map every existing URL, keep the ones that rank, and set up permanent redirects for anything that moves, so search engines carry your history over to the new site.

Can you guarantee my website will rank on Google?

No one honestly can. We build everything search engines and AI assistants look for: fast pages, clean structure, schema markup, local pages and a complete Google Business Profile. If your new site brings no inquiries in its first 90 days, we audit it and rebuild the homepage at no cost.

Do you only work with Houston businesses?

We're based in Houston and know the local market well, but we build for service businesses across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia.
