Six supporting pages that match.

Services, About, Contact, plus three of your choice (FAQ, Pricing, Case Studies, Process, individual service pages, a location page). Designed to the same standard as the homepage. Not template variants, real pages with real intent.

  • Inner pages designed page-by-page, not auto-generated
  • Consistent typography, spacing, and component system across all pages
  • Mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts hand-checked
$1,200
03

Strategic copywriting on every page.

Not lorem ipsum, not your old copy reformatted. We write every page from scratch after a 60-minute interview with you. Voice, story, objections, proof, all stitched together as a deliberate sales argument.

  • 60-minute discovery interview, transcribed
  • Headline, sub-head, and CTA framework for every page
  • Two rounds of copy revisions included
$900
04

Technical SEO done properly.

Most "SEO included" lines mean someone added meta titles. We do the boring valuable stuff: schema markup for your business type, semantic HTML, sitemap, robots, internal linking strategy, Core Web Vitals optimization, image compression pipeline.

  • LocalBusiness or specialized schema (Dentist, Attorney, etc.)
  • FAQPage and Service schema where appropriate
  • Page-speed tuning until you score 90+ on PageSpeed Insights
$650
05

AI search optimization (new).

When prospects ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overview for recommendations, the LLM picks a handful of businesses to mention. We structure your site so you're in that handful. This is the most underpriced piece of SEO work right now.

  • Content structured for LLM citation, not just keyword ranking
  • llms.txt file and AI-bot allowlist properly configured
  • Pages written so excerpts can be quoted verbatim by AI assistants
$500
06

Conversion infrastructure.

A clear path to "yes." Calendar booking system, lead-capture form with anti-spam, Google Analytics 4, conversion tracking, email-receipt setup, and CRM integration if you use one (HubSpot, Pipedrive, GoHighLevel, etc.).

  • Calendly, Cal.com, or Acuity integration
  • Form-to-email and form-to-CRM routing tested end-to-end
  • GA4 events for every meaningful interaction
$450
07

Hosting, domain, and security setup.

We handle every annoying technical thing. Domain DNS, SSL certificate, hosting on a fast platform (Cloudflare, Vercel, or Webflow/Framer hosting), security headers, automatic backups, uptime monitoring. You receive a one-page summary of every login.

  • HTTPS, HSTS, and proper redirects from day one
  • Cloudflare CDN configured for global speed
  • Automated daily backups for 30 days post-launch
$400

  • Optional maintenance retainer after the 90 days if you want to keep it going
