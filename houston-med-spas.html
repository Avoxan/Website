Questions

What med spa owners ask before booking.

The honest answers. If yours isn't here, ask it on the audit call.

Does it really book into JaneApp or Zenoti?
Yes. That's the whole point. Qualified clients get booked straight into the calendar you already run, no copy-paste, no separate system, no leads stuck in a DM folder. If you use a different platform, tell us on the call and we'll confirm whether we can connect it.
Will it sound like a robot?
No. We train it on how your spa actually talks, your tone, your treatment names, your way of explaining things. The goal is that a client feels looked after, not handed off to a clunky bot. You approve the voice before it ever goes live.
Is client and patient information safe?
We configure the front desk to keep conversations to scheduling and general information. It's set up to avoid collecting sensitive medical details, anything clinical gets routed to your staff instead of captured by the assistant. We'll walk through exactly how this is handled on the audit call so it fits your spa's privacy practices.
Do I have to replace my whole website?
The bundle includes a new, premium site designed to convert, because the front desk works best when the whole experience is built around booking. If you love your current site, say so on the call and we'll talk through options.
What does it cost?
It depends on your menu, your tools, and how much we're building. That's exactly what the free audit is for, we'll show you what it'd take and what it could recover before you commit to anything. No surprise invoices, ever.
How long until it's live?
About four weeks from kickoff, audit, then we learn your menu, build and connect everything, and launch. You'll know the exact timeline before we start.
