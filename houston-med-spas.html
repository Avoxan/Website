Questions

Asked by owners.

How much does a med spa website cost?

Our Med Spa Package website is $3,200, one time, with up to six treatment pages, copywriting, SEO and 90 days of edits. The AI front desk is $397 a month, and the first two months are free when you build the website with us.

Will the AI give medical advice to my clients?

No. It answers general questions from the information you approve, like pricing, downtime and aftercare, and books consultations. It doesn't diagnose, recommend treatments for a medical condition, or collect medical history. Anything clinical is passed to your licensed staff.

Does it work with Jane, Zenoti or Boulevard?

Yes. Website booking buttons link straight to your existing booking system, whichever one you use. On the audit call we'll walk through how the front desk books with your specific setup.

Can it answer in Spanish?

Yes. It replies in English or Spanish, depending on how the client writes or speaks, which matters for a lot of Houston clinics.

Will clients know they're talking to an AI?

It's warm and natural, and it answers in your spa's name. If a client asks, it says it's the spa's AI assistant, and it can always pass the conversation to a person.

Do I have to use your AI front desk to get the website?

No. The website stands on its own. Most spas add the front desk because after-hours inquiries are where they lose the most bookings, but it's your call.

Will my website rank for “med spa near me” in Houston?

We build every treatment page to rank, set up your Google Business Profile and local schema, and write content for the neighborhoods you serve, like River Oaks, The Heights, Memorial, Katy or The Woodlands. No one can promise a #1 spot, but local rankings typically improve within three to six months.
